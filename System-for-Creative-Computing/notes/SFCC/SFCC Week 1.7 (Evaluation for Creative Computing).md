## Gesture-Controlled Music App Heuristics (Nielsen-Style)

``` text

1. Clear feedback when a gesture is detected.  
2. Gestures should match the action they trigger.  
3. Keep latency low and consistent.  
4. Gestures must be easy to tell apart.  
5. Recognition should work reliably in different conditions.  
6. Feedback should prevent users repeating gestures.  
7. Provide simple onboarding or calibration.  
8. Show how the system sees the user’s hand.  
9. Gestures should feel smooth and expressive.  
10. Users shouldn’t need to constantly look at the screen.

```
